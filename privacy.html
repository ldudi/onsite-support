Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

OnSite is designed to help users create and manage estimates, invoices, customers, saved items, photos, and related business documents on iPhone.

Data Collection

OnSite does not require an account and does not collect your business records on our servers.

Business information, customer details, estimates, invoices, saved items, notes, and related records are stored locally on your device.

OnSite does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Photos

You may choose photos from your device to attach to documents. Photos are accessed only when you explicitly select them using Apple's system photo picker.

Selected photos are used as part of your local document workflow and may be included in PDFs when you choose.

Document Sharing

OnSite allows estimates and invoices to be exported and shared as PDF files.

Information leaves the app only when you choose to share or export content using iOS sharing features. The service or application you select may process that content according to its own privacy policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your records remain stored locally on your device until you edit or delete them, remove the app, or otherwise remove the associated local data.

Because OnSite does not maintain user accounts or a remote database containing your business records, there is no server-side account containing those records to request for deletion.

Third Parties

OnSite does not sell your personal information and does not share your business records with advertising or analytics providers.

Subscriptions and Purchases

OnSite uses Apple's StoreKit to offer optional subscriptions. Purchases and subscription transactions are processed by Apple. OnSite does not receive or store your payment card details on developer-controlled servers.

OnSite may store your current subscription entitlement and expiration date locally on your device so subscribed features remain available, including when temporarily offline. Apple's handling of transaction information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy .

Children

OnSite is a general business productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if OnSite's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact
